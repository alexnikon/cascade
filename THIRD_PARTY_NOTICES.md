## AmneziaWG Architect

Source: <https://github.com/Vadim-Khristenko/Any-Tech-ARCHITECT>

Pinned source: Any-Tech-ARCHITECT v4.5.1, commit
`0b9d3b7aa6c1661daa29b92010f841a3c04ca9fb`.

Cascade ports the AWG 2.0/3.1 generator to Go and embeds its client,
fingerprint and host-pool catalogues. Cascade owns the shared header-protection
key even for Amnezia VPN because the template configures both tunnel ends.
